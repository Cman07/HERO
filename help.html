    How HERO uses your information

    Your completed questionnaire answers are kept in this browser tab so you can continue after a refresh. For nonemergency chats, your messages and any survey answers are sent to Azure AI. Urgent guidance is handled without AI. This guide does not save chat history. If you opt in above after completing the survey, only saved home ZIP and household size are included. Saved health, disability, and other support answers stay local. The chat cannot verify your location or check eligibility, live conditions, or application availability.
